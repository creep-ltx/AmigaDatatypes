# amiansi.datatype changes

## 0.1b1 (17.10.2026)

- First version: Amiga ANSI art (BBS screens, `.ans`, Amiga `.asc`/`.nfo`/`.diz`) in MultiView and every
  datatypes program, in the scene's fonts (Topaz 1/1+/2/2+, MicroKnight/+, P0T-NOoDLE, mO'sOul: by SAUCE,
  Topaz 1+ without), the Amiga ANSI colours, italic and underline, on black.
- Animations play once at modem speed (`ENV:ANSIBaud`, 14,400 by default, 0: never), then hold.
- Built from pcansi.datatype's source with -DAMIANSI; the two never take the same file.
